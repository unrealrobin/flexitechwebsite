Serving Venice

Venice's Local Pool Building Experts

Venice presents a different challenge than most of our service area — narrower lots, tighter side-yard access for equipment and material delivery, and, near the canals, higher water tables that change how a pool is engineered. We've built pools across all of these conditions and plan accordingly from the first site visit.

The neighborhood's architecture has shifted heavily toward clean, modern design over the last two decades, and we design pools to match — sharp geometric shapes, integrated spas, sunken lounges, and minimalist tile and coping that complement contemporary construction.

Compact lots don't mean compromise. Plunge pools, cocktail pools, and narrow lap pools let Venice homeowners get a real pool experience even on some of the tighter lots near the beach and the canals.

Common Questions from Venice Homeowners

My lot is narrow with tight side-yard access. Can you still build a pool?

Yes — this is common in Venice, and we plan for it from day one. We assess equipment and material access during the initial site visit and adjust our construction approach, sometimes using smaller equipment or crane access, to work within tight lot constraints.

Does being near the canals or the beach affect pool construction?

It can affect soil conditions and water table depth, which our engineers account for in the structural design. We'll flag any site-specific considerations during your estimate so there are no surprises once construction starts.

How long does it take to get a pool permit in Venice / Los Angeles?

Permit approval through the City of Los Angeles typically takes 6–10 weeks for a standard pool project. We submit all permit applications on your behalf and follow up with the city throughout the process, so there are no surprises to your project schedule.

Do you offer financing or payment plans?

We can refer you to third-party financing partners that specialize in home improvement projects. Many homeowners use home equity loans or lines of credit for pool construction. We'll walk through payment structure and milestone-based payments at the time of your estimate.
