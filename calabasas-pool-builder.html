Common Questions from Calabasas Homeowners

What types of pools are most popular in Calabasas?

Calabasas homeowners tend to gravitate toward resort-style designs — pool and spa combinations, outdoor kitchens, fire features, and lush hardscape that create a complete backyard retreat. We also see strong demand for contemporary geometric pools with clean lines to complement modern architecture.

How much does a custom pool cost in Calabasas?

Custom pool pricing varies based on size, design complexity, and features. Most projects in Calabasas range from $80,000 to $200,000+ when including the pool, spa, and surrounding hardscape. We provide a detailed, itemized written estimate after visiting your property — no vague ballparks.

Do you work within Calabasas HOA guidelines?

Yes. We're familiar with the HOA requirements common in Calabasas communities and handle all permit and approval paperwork on your behalf. We've built in gated communities throughout the area and know how to navigate the process efficiently.

Can you add a spa or water features to an existing pool?

Absolutely. Pool remodels and additions are a significant part of our work. We can add an in-ground spa, waterfall, fire feature, or completely refinish and update an existing pool. We'll assess your current pool and give you an honest plan for what's possible.
