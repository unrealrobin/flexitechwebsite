Common Questions from Pacific Palisades Homeowners

Are you experienced building pools on hillside and sloped lots?

Yes — hillside pool construction is one of our specialties. Sloped and hillside lots require additional engineering for retaining walls, drainage, and structural support. We handle the full scope of this work in-house, including any required engineering drawings and geological assessments.

How does an infinity pool actually work?

An infinity pool creates the illusion of water extending to the horizon by having one or more edges where the water flows over into a hidden catch basin below. The water is then recirculated back into the pool. Proper engineering of the weir wall height, catch basin volume, and pump capacity is critical — done incorrectly, the effect fails and the pool can experience water loss. Our team has built dozens of infinity pools and understands this system inside and out.

Do you need special permits for hillside pool construction in Pacific Palisades?

Yes — hillside construction in LA often requires additional geological reports, soils engineering, and structural engineering in addition to standard pool permits. We handle all of this as part of our project management, coordinating with the necessary engineers and the City of Los Angeles Building and Safety department.

What are the lead times for starting a pool project in Pacific Palisades?

We typically schedule consultations within one week of your inquiry. After a signed contract, the permitting process in LA can take 6–12 weeks. Construction itself takes 2–4 months. We'll give you an accurate timeline specific to your project at our consultation.
