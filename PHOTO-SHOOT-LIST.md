# Flexi Tech Website — Full Photo Shoot List

A complete list of every photo needed to replace the placeholder images across all pages.
Organized by shoot type so you can capture everything efficiently in one or two sessions.

---

## How to Replace a Photo

Once you have a photo ready, save it to the correct folder with the filename listed below.
The website will automatically use it — no code changes needed.

All photos go inside the **`images/`** folder (or **`images/gallery/`** for gallery photos).

## Photography Tips for Pool Photos

**Best time to shoot:** Early morning (soft light, no glare) or golden hour (1 hour before sunset). Avoid midday — harsh shadows and glare on water.

**Blue hour tip:** The hour after sunset with the pool lights on is the most dramatic look for luxury pools. The sky goes deep blue, the water glows, and it looks like a magazine shoot.

**Equipment:** A wide-angle lens (16–24mm on full frame) works best for pools. A drone for aerial shots of the full backyard is worth it for at least 2–3 hero shots.

**Water:** Make sure the pool is freshly cleaned, water is clear, and there are no floats, toys, or debris. Remove pool equipment from the shot if possible.

**Staging:** A few clean lounge chairs, a rolled towel, and a plant or two make a big visual difference. Keep it minimal and aspirational.
