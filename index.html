Common Questions

What Homeowners Ask Before They Build

Straight answers, before you ever pick up the phone.

How much does a custom pool cost in Los Angeles?

Most custom pools we build in the LA and San Fernando Valley area range from the mid-five to low-six figures, depending on size, finishes, and features like spas or water elements. We give every client a detailed, itemized written estimate before any work begins — no vague ballpark numbers.

How long does it take to build a pool?

A typical project runs 2–4 months from groundbreaking to final walkthrough, depending on scope, permitting timelines, and weather. We give you a realistic schedule up front and keep you updated at every phase.

Do you handle permits and engineering?

Yes. Flexi Tech manages all permits, engineering drawings, and city approvals in-house, so you're not stuck navigating LA's permitting process on your own.

What if my contractor disappears mid-project — how are you different?

Ken personally oversees every job from start to finish, and we're licensed, bonded, and insured. We've built 200+ pools since 1998 with consistent crews and direct owner involvement — not a rotating cast of subcontractors.

Do you offer financing?

We can walk you through financing options during your free consultation and help you find a plan that fits your budget and timeline.
