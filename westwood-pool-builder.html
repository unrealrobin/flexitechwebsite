Westwood's Local Pool Building Experts

Westwood is often thought of as a UCLA-adjacent neighborhood, but its residential streets — particularly toward the Holmby Hills border and Comstock area — include some of the more established, generously sized lots on the Westside. We build accordingly, with an eye toward both scale and privacy.

We're experienced navigating the mix of older and newer construction in Westwood, and we design pools that work with the existing footprint of the property — whether that means fitting a pool into a more traditional layout or integrating it into a recently remodeled contemporary home.

As with our other Westside projects, we bring premium finishes, automation, and lighting as standard, and we're comfortable coordinating with architects and landscape designers already engaged on a broader property renovation.

Common Questions from Westwood Homeowners

How long does it take to get a pool permit in Westwood / Los Angeles?

Permit approval through the City of Los Angeles typically takes 6–10 weeks for a standard pool project. We submit all permit applications on your behalf and follow up with the city throughout the process, so there are no surprises to your project schedule.

Can you coordinate with our architect or landscape designer?

Yes, we regularly collaborate with architects, interior designers, and landscape architects on Westside projects. If you have existing design plans, we can work directly from them and integrate our pool construction with a broader renovation.

What premium finishes do you offer?

We work with the full range of premium finishes including Pebble Tec, Pebble Fina, Pebble Sheen, glass tile, and hand-laid mosaic tile, along with travertine, limestone, and natural stone coping. We'll help you select finishes that complement your home's architecture.

Do you offer financing or payment plans?

We can refer you to third-party financing partners that specialize in home improvement projects. Many homeowners use home equity loans or lines of credit for pool construction. We'll walk through payment structure and milestone-based payments at the time of your estimate.
