Brentwood's Trusted Pool Building Partner

Common Questions from Brentwood Homeowners

How long does it take to get a pool permit in Brentwood / Los Angeles?

Permit approval through the City of Los Angeles typically takes 6–10 weeks for a standard pool project. We submit all permit applications on your behalf and follow up with the city throughout the process, so there are no surprises to your project schedule.

Can you help with privacy landscaping around the pool area?

Yes. Many Brentwood lots benefit from added privacy screening — hedging, trellis walls, or water features that add ambient sound. We can design these elements alongside your pool or coordinate with your landscape architect.

Can you remodel an older pool without disturbing the rest of my yard?

In most cases, yes. We stage equipment and material delivery carefully and can often complete a remodel with minimal disruption to surrounding landscaping, hardscape, and mature trees.

Do you offer financing or payment plans?

We can refer you to third-party financing partners that specialize in home improvement projects. Many homeowners use home equity loans or lines of credit for pool construction. We'll walk through payment structure and milestone-based payments at the time of your estimate.
