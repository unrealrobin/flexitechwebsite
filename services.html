Pool & Backyard Services

Every service we offer is designed to transform your outdoor space into something extraordinary.

What We Offer

Complete Pool & Outdoor Living Solutions

From the first shovel to the final finish, Flexi Tech handles every stage of your project in-house. No subcontractor runaround — just one team, fully accountable, start to finish.

Service 01

Custom Pool Construction

There is no such thing as a standard backyard, and we never build a standard pool. Every project begins with a conversation about your lifestyle, your space, and your vision. We then design a pool that fits seamlessly into your property — whether that's a sleek lap pool, a freeform lagoon style, or a geometric showpiece.

Our in-house team handles excavation, plumbing, shotcrete shell construction, waterproofing, tile, coping, plaster finish, and equipment installation. We also manage all engineering drawings and city permits so you don't have to.

  • Design, engineering, and permits included
  • Gunite/shotcrete shell construction
  • Premium plaster, Pebble Tec, and tile finishes
  • Energy-efficient pump and filtration systems

Service 02

Spas & Hot Tubs

A custom spa built by Flexi Tech is more than a hot tub — it's a permanent retreat. We design and construct in-ground spas that flow naturally alongside your pool or stand alone as a dramatic focal point in your landscape.

Choose from jetted therapy spas, overflow spas, elevated spas with water features, or integrated pool-spa combos. We'll match materials, finishes, and lighting to create a seamless, resort-quality result.

  • In-ground gunite spa construction
  • Therapy jets and hydrotherapy options
  • Integrated heating and automation systems
  • Overflow and waterfall spa designs

Service 03

Infinity & Zero-Edge Pools

One of the most visually dramatic pool styles, zero-edge and infinity pools create a breathtaking visual effect where the water appears to merge with the horizon, a canyon view, or surrounding landscape. We are specialists in this technically demanding style of construction.

Proper engineering and precise execution are everything with infinity pools. Our team has the expertise to ensure the catch basin, weir wall, and recirculation system are built to flawless standards — delivering the stunning visual effect you're after, for decades to come.

  • Precision weir wall and catch basin engineering
  • Infinity-style and partial zero-edge designs
  • Hillside and view-lot specialists
  • Seamless integration with deck and landscape

Service 04

Pool Remodeling & Renovation

Your existing pool deserves a second life. Whether it's worn plaster, cracked coping, outdated tile, or inefficient equipment, Flexi Tech can fully renovate your pool to modern standards — often without the cost or timeline of a full rebuild.

We assess your existing pool structure, identify what can be restored, and give you an honest plan that maximizes your investment. Many clients are amazed at how transformative a remodel can be.

  • Plaster and Pebble Tec refinishing
  • Tile and coping replacement
  • Equipment upgrades (pumps, heaters, automation)
  • LED lighting installation and automation

Service 05

Water Features & Fountains

The sound of flowing water transforms a backyard from a place to escape into a place to truly unwind. Flexi Tech designs and builds custom water features that enhance any pool or stand alone as a dramatic landscape focal point.

From cascading rock waterfalls and sheer descent falls to fire-and-water features, bubblers, and custom fountains — we create water features that become the signature element of your outdoor space.

  • Natural rock and grotto waterfalls
  • Sheer descent and deck jets
  • Fire-and-water feature integration
  • Standalone fountain construction
Get a Quote
Service 06

Decking & Hardscape Design

A stunning pool deserves a stunning surround. Our hardscape team designs and builds pool decks, patios, walkways, retaining walls, outdoor kitchens, and pergolas that complete your outdoor living space and tie the entire backyard together.

We work with a wide range of materials including natural stone, travertine, concrete, pavers, and tile — selecting what best suits your aesthetic, climate, and budget while ensuring durability for the LA climate.

  • Travertine, stone, and paver deck installation
  • Stamped and decorative concrete
  • Retaining walls and outdoor structures
  • Outdoor kitchen and BBQ areas
