Common Questions from Sherman Oaks Homeowners

How long does it take to get a pool permit in Sherman Oaks / Los Angeles?

Permit approval through the City of Los Angeles typically takes 6–10 weeks for a standard pool project. We submit all permit applications on your behalf and follow up with the city throughout the process, so there are no surprises to your project schedule.

Can you remodel my existing pool in Sherman Oaks?

Yes — pool remodeling is one of our most common services in Sherman Oaks, where many homes have pools built in the 1960s through 80s that are ready for an upgrade. We can resurface plaster, replace tile and coping, upgrade equipment, and add features like a spa or LED lighting.

My backyard isn't very large. Can you still build a pool?

Absolutely. Smaller backyards are one of our strengths — we're skilled at designing pools that maximize a compact space, including plunge pools, cocktail pools, and narrow lap pools.

Do you offer financing or payment plans?

We can refer you to third-party financing partners that specialize in home improvement projects. Many homeowners use home equity loans or lines of credit for pool construction. We'll walk through payment structure and milestone-based payments at the time of your estimate.
