Common Questions from Hidden Hills Homeowners

Do you handle permits for pool construction in Hidden Hills?

Yes — we handle all permit applications, engineering drawings, and city approvals on your behalf. Hidden Hills has specific HOA and city guidelines for construction, and our team is experienced navigating them.

How long does a custom pool build typically take in Hidden Hills?

Most custom pool builds take between 2 and 4 months from permit approval to completion. The timeline depends on the complexity of the design, site conditions, and any additional features like spas, water features, or hardscape work.

What pool styles work best on Hidden Hills estate lots?

Hidden Hills properties lend themselves beautifully to naturalistic designs — think freeform pools with rock water features, grottos, and lush landscaping. We also build sleek contemporary pools for newly constructed modern estates. We'll assess your property and present options that enhance your specific setting.

Is Flexi Tech licensed and insured to work in Hidden Hills?

Absolutely. Flexi Tech Contracting Inc. is fully licensed, bonded, and insured in the State of California. We carry all required contractor licenses and liability insurance for every project we take on.
