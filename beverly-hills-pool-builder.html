Common Questions from Beverly Hills Homeowners

What premium pool finishes do you offer?

We work with the full range of premium finishes including Pebble Tec, Pebble Fina, Pebble Sheen, glass tile, hand-laid mosaic tile, and various plaster options. For coping we offer travertine, limestone, granite, and custom-cut natural stone. We'll help you select finishes that complement your home's architecture and your personal aesthetic.

Can you match my pool design to my architect's or landscape designer's plans?

Absolutely. We regularly collaborate with architects, interior designers, and landscape architects on high-end projects. If you have existing design plans or renderings, we can work directly from them. We're experienced integrating our pool construction with broader landscape and hardscape projects.

Do you offer pool automation and smart home integration?

Yes. We install Pentair and Jandy automation systems that control your pool's temperature, lighting, jets, and water features from a smartphone app. We can also integrate with smart home systems like Control4 or Crestron for full home automation compatibility.

How do I get started on a pool project in Beverly Hills?

Contact us to schedule a free consultation. We'll visit your property, discuss your vision and budget, and follow up with a detailed design concept and written estimate. There's no pressure and no obligation.
